# Traty — privacy policy

The published privacy policy for the Traty Android app, and the account
deletion page it links to. This repository exists only to serve those
documents at a stable public address, as Google Play requires: a privacy
policy link has to be both in Play Console and inside the app.

- Privacy policy: https://charly3x.github.io/traty-privacy/
- Deleting your account: https://charly3x.github.io/traty-privacy/delete-account.html

**English only.** A policy is a legal document, and two translations under one
name drift apart the moment a correction lands in one of them. The Russian
pages were published until 23 August 2026 and have been removed; the app has
always linked to the English edition.

The source of truth is `docs/privacy-policy.en.md` and
`docs/account-deletion.en.md` in the app's own repository; the pages here are
generated from them. Change the text there, regenerate, and push.
