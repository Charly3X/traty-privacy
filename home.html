Traty

Traty reads a paper receipt from a photograph and sorts what you bought into categories, so a month of spending is a few numbers instead of a drawer full of paper. Receipts can also be typed in by hand, and that stays free however much you use it.

The app runs on Android. Recognition happens on our server rather than on the phone, and the photograph of the receipt is not stored anywhere — the privacy policy below says exactly what is sent, to whom, and for how long anything is kept.
